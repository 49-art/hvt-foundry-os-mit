============================================================
WHAT THIS PRACTICE IS
============================================================
A high-street or parade-room practice: physio, dentist
rooms, osteopath, aesthetic clinic, counselling rooms,
GP-adjacent private rooms. Time is the stock. Trust is
the product. Paper diaries still exist.

Typical picture
- Practitioner + receptionist, or practitioner alone
- Slots on a wall calendar or a branded diary app
- Recall letters they never send
- No-shows cost more than software
- They are frightened of “patient data in a new app”

Classification
  Sector:          Services
  Market Industry: Local health & care rooms
  Business Type:   Local clinic / practitioner
  Business Model:  Appointment + fee. Packages / courses
                   of treatment common.

============================================================
PROBLEMS
============================================================
  PRB-0024  No-shows and empty slots
  PRB-0025  Recall / “you’re due back” never goes out
  PRB-0026  Diary in one place, payments in another,
            notes in a third — or on paper
  PRB-0027  New patients cannot book without phoning
            during the appointment they are interrupting
  PRB-0028  Reviews sit unanswered; fear of one bad star

============================================================
SHELF (show all)
============================================================
  Tier 4: the diary they already use; the notes system
          they are legally tied to; their card machine
  Tier 1: Google Calendar; GBP; WhatsApp Business app
          (human, not clinical advice on WhatsApp)
  Tier 2: ENG-0002 booking + reminders
          ENG-0001 people / recall list
          ENG-0003 payments / packages
          DR-10 review path — owner replies, no auto-clinical
  Tier 3: Cliniko, Jane, Mindbody, Fresha, Dentally,
          Phorest-class clinic suites — alternatives on
          the shelf. Do not rip them out if already owned.
  Never:  Foundry-hosted clinical notes as a “feature”

============================================================
GAPS

MINIMUM PATH

  Payments on the account they already have.
