### WHAT THIS TRADE IS

A carpenter, joiner, small builder, kitchen fitter, or similar: van, phone, tape measure, one or two mates. Jobs live in WhatsApp. Quotes live in a notebook. The website they were sold does not get them work.

Typical picture
- Job is a place, not a till
- Deposit then balance
- Materials from a merchant account
- Rain and “the other trade didn’t show” destroy the diary
- Photos of the job ARE the brand

Classification Sector: Trades Market Industry: Building & joinery Business Type: Carpenter / small building trade Business Model: Quoted job + deposit + schedule. Some day-rate. Some small retail of offcuts / furniture if they have a shop.

### PROBLEMS

- PRB-0029 Quotes take a night and then go cold PRB-0030 Diary vs materials vs who is on site PRB-0031 Deposit taken, extras argued later PRB-0032 Photos trapped on the phone PRB-0033 No idea which jobs made money

### SHELF (show all)

Tier 4: WhatsApp, merchant account, van insurer, existing bank / SumUp / Stripe Tier 1: Google Calendar; Google Drive for job photos; spreadsheet job list; open-source job-ticket tools if they will actually open them Tier 2: ENG-0001 customers / sites ENG-0002 slots for survey / fit days ENG-0003 deposits and balances procedure: quote → accept → deposit → schedule Tier 3: ServiceTitan, Jobber, Buildertrend, Houzz Pro, Checkatrade / MyBuilder as lead doors — on the shelf as options, not as “join this or you don’t exist” Stock of timber / ironmongery: DR-10 path (merchant account + sheet). ### GAPS

### MINIMUM PATH

One job record: who, where, quote, deposit, dates. Calendar they already have. Payments they already have. Drive folder per job for photos. GBP with real photos of THEIR work.
