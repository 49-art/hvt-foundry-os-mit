## Decision

ENG-0003 implied ENG-0001 and ENG-0002 existed, but neither was ever logged in the Asset Registry. Architecture Office confirms this was an accidental numbering gap, not a reference to undisclosed prior work. The gap is closed by reserving and defining two new Engines, chosen to be composable with ENG-0003 and to serve the Foundry’s restated objective: practical, instantly integrable solutions for high-street businesses.

## Action Taken

Registered two new Engine assets in HVT-0003 and foundry_graph.json:

| Asset ID | Name | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| ENG-0001 | Local Customer Engine | Simple CRM + appointment book + basic loyalty for a single-location high-street business | Prototype |
| ENG-0002 | Local Content & Booking Engine | Simple website + online booking + local map integration, editable by a non-technical owner from a phone | Prototype |

Both are logged at Prototype status. Per HVT-0001 Art. III.3.a, promotion to Reusable Asset requires use in at least two distinct real high-street projects — the same bar ENG-0003 has already cleared.

## Objective Filter Applied

Both engines were checked against the high-street filter before being logged:

1. Solves a concrete daily problem a high-street owner has (customer records/appointments; a website and booking page they can actually maintain).
2. A non-technical owner can approve and go live in minutes, not weeks.
3. Each leaves a reusable Business Asset behind, reducing cost and time for the next high-street client.

## Rationale

Per HVT-0001 Art. V, no silent gaps are permitted in the numbering or the record. Per Art. XIII, the Foundry accumulates Business Assets; leaving ENG-0001/ENG-0002 unallocated indefinitely would contradict that mandate once real high-street demand for customer management and local booking was identified. Defining them now, at Prototype status, logs the intent without overstating their maturity.

## Authority

Issued under HVT-0001 Art. V and Art. XIII, and HVT-0002 §3.2 (Architecture Office decision authority over the Engine Registry).
