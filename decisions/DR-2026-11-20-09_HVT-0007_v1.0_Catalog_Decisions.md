## Decisions

1. **Integration vs Solution Component: Solution Component replaces Integration.** INT-0001…0007 stay in the record, unedited, with status **Superseded**; each is linked from its SLC by `supersedes`. No new INT- IDs will be issued. ("Permanent subtype" was the alternative; it is rejected because it keeps two schemas for one thing.)
2. **"Official" partner status: retired.** On the Stripe example it meant "the vendor's own public API", not a vendor-verified partnership. **SLC-0001 Stripe partner_status = Public Integration**, the same as the six other vendors with no partnership agreement. "Verified" is kept for a real vendor verification of the Foundry.
3. **SLC numbering confirmed as in the graph:** SLC-0001 Stripe, SLC-0002 Square, SLC-0003 Google Calendar, SLC-0004 Apple Calendar, SLC-0005 WhatsApp Business, SLC-0006 Xero, SLC-0007 QuickBooks (the "provisional" label is removed from the ID; every field stays unverified), SLC-0008 Apple Pay. HVT-0007 §5.2's "SLC-0002–SLC-0006" is a typo for SLC-0002–SLC-0007.
4. **DR-2026-09-25-04 fact-check applied to the records:** Stripe 46 countries (the 170+ figure stays only on the Superseded INT-0001); Apple Pay 101 countries and regions (Apple list, 9 Sep 2026); Xero UK payroll included from the Grow plan, not an add-on. Anything not checked stays labelled unverified.
5. **Referral income:** CommercialAgreement (CAG-) record, owned by Commercial, with rates set by Finance and the CEO Office (HVT-0004 v1.0). It never appears as a catalog field or in the room.
6. **Not registered (candidates only until verified against the vendor's own pages):** the five further Apple products (Wallet, Maps, Business Connect, Sign in with Apple, Push Notifications), Pronto, and every Tier-3 name on the cards (Fresha, Booksy, Treatwell, Phorest, Salon Iris, Cliniko, Jane, Mindbody, Dentally, Shopify POS, Lightspeed, Toast, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Buildertrend, Houzz Pro, Checkatrade, MyBuilder). The ladder shows them on the shelf as **"unverified candidate"**. Art. VI.4 keeps them visible.
