## Decisions

1. **HVT-0004 v1.0 Approved.** Everything proposed in §2.1, §4.1 and §5.1 of the v0.2/v0.3 text is **canonical**: Sector, Market Industry, Business Type, Business Model, Problem, Integration, Solution Component, Customer Session, Customer Wish, Workflow, plus the four tiers and partner status.
2. **ID prefixes in use (canonical list):** HVT-, ENG-, PKG-, DOC-, IND- (Industry Template), DR-, WKF-, SEC-, MKT-, BUT-, BUM-, PRB-, INT- (closed, historical), SLC-, SES-, WSH-, **AGT-** (adopted), **STI-** (adopted), **CAG-** (new), **EXC-** (new, Exceptions under Art. XII).
3. **Lifecycle vocabularies for the classes that had none:**
   - Sector, Market Industry, Business Model, Problem, Solution Component: **Registered → Superseded**.
   - Integration: **Registered → Superseded**. All INT-0001…0007 are now Superseded by their SLC (DR-2026-11-20-09).
   - Business Type: **Registered → Approved → Superseded**. Approved = the card is approved as classification; it is not a live instance.
   - Documentation assets: `status` carries **asset maturity** (Prototype → … → Reusable Asset), and `document_status` carries the document lifecycle (Draft / Approved / Superseded). This resolves the "Draft as an asset status" discrepancy (DR-2026-09-25-01). The kernel still accepts the legacy value "Draft" on Assets for older data.
   - Customer Wish: **no new status.** An out-of-scope wish stays "Under Review" with resolution text "Out of scope: …". This is how the Studio already works (DR-2026-11-20-11).
4. **StockItem (STI-####) adopted** as the schema in DR-2026-09-04-11 §4, with the edges belongs_to Owner, sourced_from SourceSystem, alerts_via Connector. **There are no instances** until ENG-0005 registers on its trigger (DR-2026-11-20-05).
5. **CommercialAgreement (CAG-####) adopted** for referral income and partner terms. It is referenced by a Solution Component, never embedded in one. Owner: Commercial, with Finance for rates. **No instances.**
6. **Business Model:** a `business_model` field on the BusinessType card (prose). A BUM-#### object is minted only when one type needs structural variants that change Workflows or Packages. The first likely case is BUT-0004 (repair vs walk-in).
7. **complexity_score method (1–5):** `1 + the number of required capabilities with no registered Foundry Engine (even when a connector path exists) + 1 if any capability on the card is out of scope by law (e.g. clinical records)`, capped at 5. It is computed by `foundry_os` from the graph, not typed in by hand. BUT-0001 = 1 + 1 (stock) = **2**, the same as the original estimate.
8. **New relationship `family_of`** (Problem → Problem). It records overlaps without minting duplicates, and without merging IDs that already exist (IDs are never reused).
9. **Multi-type instances:** an instance carries `primary_business_type` and optional `also_business_types`. The Configurator matches on the primary and unions the problems of the others (DR-2026-11-20-08).
10. **Storage (§8):** file-based JSON stays (graph/foundry_graph.json). It is validated by the kernel on every boot.
