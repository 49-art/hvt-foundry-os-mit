## 2. HVT-0001 → v1.3 Approved (Article XI amendment)

**Proposal (Art. XI.1.a).** Article affected: XIV. Change: add 14.6. Rationale: the principle "the customer's stated reality overrides any prior classification" is repeated as practice in DR-2026-08-04-06, HVT-0006 §9 and DOC-0005, but it was never codified (DR-2026-08-04-08 open follow-on).

> **14.6** The customer's stated reality overrides any prior classification. Where an owner's own account of their business, its problems or their wishes contradicts the Foundry's classification, the classification is reopened: the business is re-classified, a Customer Wish is recorded, or a new Business Type is flagged. The owner is never fitted to a card. The owner's words are recorded verbatim. The Foundry may record its disagreement in writing, but may not overwrite those words. This clause does not oblige the Foundry to build what is asked: Article II still governs whether anything new is built.
