## 2. How the line was drawn (plain English)

- **The founder can approve.** He owns the Foundry ("Foundry ownership", HVT-0001 Art. XI.1.b). The CEO Office is the top of the chain (HVT-0002 §3.1). Its job includes approving the Foundry's governance documents (HVT-0002 §4.2). So the founder's approval covers every Draft whose rules don't name anyone else.
- **Where a document names a person, that person still has to sign.** Some documents say they only become live with one named person's sign-off: Raj's stamp on DOC-0008, Raj on the overlay, Raj and Stan Lee on BUT-0005/6/7, and Stan Lee's design veto. For these, the founder's approval is recorded, but the item stays **"Approved by Founder; pending <that person>"**. Nobody's stamp has been copied or invented.
- **Where the rules ask for the Chief Architect, and nobody holds that role.** HVT-0002 §4.2 lists the Chief Architect as a role, but no person is named for it anywhere in the record. The working list shows only drafting agents in the Architecture Office. In those cases the founder's approval **stands as interim**, and the Chief Architect review is still owed. Those items are flagged below.
- **Decision records are not edited.** HVT-0001 Art. V.4 says a decision record can't be changed once it's logged. So the older Draft decision records keep their own text. This record is the entry that carries their approval.
- **Who did the sorting.** Grok Bot applied the written rules to sort each item. Travis can move any line, and this record is the place to do it (HVT-0001 Art. IV.2.c: a person can accept, reject or change AI work).
- **Nothing was deleted.** Each changed document got a new version number, a new status line, its old status kept on the line below, and a new changelog row. The text was not changed. HVT-0003 and HVT-0004 already had separate Draft files, so each got a new file and the old files stay.
