## 2. Overlaps: shared IDs, or new IDs linked by family_of

- **Shared, not duplicated** (already decided on the cards): PRB-0007 is used by BUT-0002, 0003 and 0005. PRB-0005 is used by BUT-0001 and 0004.
- **Kept as their own IDs, linked with `family_of`:** PRB-0020 → PRB-0019 (empty midweek), PRB-0021 → PRB-0010 (till, sheet and book disagree), PRB-0024 → PRB-0001 and PRB-0002 (no-shows, empty slots), PRB-0028 → PRB-0006 (unanswered reviews). The rule from here on: **reuse an existing PRB when the owner's words describe the same problem. Mint a new one, linked with family_of, when the context changes what solves it** (for example, a clinic's no-show has recall and consent obligations a salon's does not).

## 3. Dual-type rule (BUT-0002 + BUT-0003, BUT-0007 + BUT-0004/0005)

**Allowed, using primary + also_business_types (HVT-0004 v1.0 §9.9).** A premises can be a CEE grocer-deli (primary) that also states trust rules (also BUT-0003). The owner's trust sentence is stored on the owner record and in the sheet's "rule / mark" column. Foundry never certifies it. A carpenter with an offcuts shop is BUT-0007 primary, also BUT-0004.

**Routing BUT-0005 vs 0002/0003/0004 (the prose rule, now fixed):** classify as 0002 if the counter is a food import; as 0003 if the owner states a trust or dietary rule as part of the offer; as 0004 if the owner's craft knowledge is the product; **otherwise** as 0005. Never double-card.
